## How I work with a team

I start with the real user workflow, agree on acceptance criteria and tradeoffs, then ship a version we can measure. I move between UI, APIs, data, and operations as the problem requires. I use AI where it helps, review its output critically, and rely on tests and production signals to decide whether the work is done.

I have shipped on teams in automotive software, healthcare, and federal data, and I have operated my own paid SaaS products. I am looking for a team that values both customer contact and engineering discipline.
